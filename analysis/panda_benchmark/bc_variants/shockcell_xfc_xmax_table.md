# Centreline shock-cell locations at \(M_j=1.42\)

All locations are extracted from the centreline mean density. The closure
location \(x_{fc}\) is obtained from the maximum positive gradient of the raw
profile. The numerical \(x_n\) values are
cell-scale maxima of the centreline mean density after a \(0.12D_e\)
moving-box filter, used here to merge subsidiary maxima within broad
recompression plateaux. A minimum prominence of \(0.02\rho_j\), a minimum
separation of \(0.5D_e\), and three-point quadratic refinement are applied.
Experimental maxima are extracted from the raw sampled mean-density profile.
