DEPOSIT INFORMATION 

Total  No. of  Files: 23 including the README.TXT  

Description:   Data used for individual figure in the paper are listed by the respective figure number. Figure 11 is from a subset of data that make a video animation for M = 1.19 jet. The complete data set is provided in FIG11DEN.DAT & FIG11PRS.DAT. A similar data set for M = 1.42 jet is also provided. Finally the video animation for M = 1.19 and 1.42 jets are provided in two .mpg format files.   Special Instructions:  You need an MPEG player to see the video. An example for PC users is  Microsoft windows media player.
