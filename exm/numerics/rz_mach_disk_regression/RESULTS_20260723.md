# Mach-disk shock regression result, 2026-07-23

## Decision

The controlled planar Mach-10 matrix passed all 15 predeclared checks, but the
real SRP checkpoint A/B failed 4 frozen acceptance checks. The
`rz_euler_consistent_shock_hybrid_v2` candidate is therefore rejected for
production use and must remain off by default.

The requested follow-up decomposition and additive H-correction have now been
implemented. The four-way control split, face-local audit, planar H calibration,
one-stage real-SRP H audit, and exact-time 250-step real-SRP gate are complete.
The additive `H=0.125` candidate formally passes every pre-result short-window
gate, but the effect is not materially distinguishable from the baseline field.
The engineering decision is therefore `NO-GO`: the candidate remains
default-off and is not promoted to a pre-defect long run.

A subsequent exact-time reproduction of the original NPR15 visual anomaly
also corrected the target definition. Its apparent 20-cell center cliff is a
shock-identity switch in the old max-gradient detector, not a deformed Mach
disk. The true supersonic-to-subsonic disk is subcell-flat in the challenged
axis core. The downstream cap remains a real computed recirculation/stagnation
compression, but it must be analyzed separately from Mach-disk geometry.

This is a legacy low-storage-RK2 checkpoint diagnostic, not an SSPRK(4,3)
production qualification. Every real-geometry branch reported
`pure_shared_gp_full_cartesian`, `cut_control_compiled=0`, full Cartesian cell
volumes and face areas, and the shared ghost-point extension. No cut-cell,
cut-control, aggregate, redistribution, or AMReX-EB flow update was used.

## Tests completed

### Controlled normal shock

The 12-case Y9000X matrix used a stationary Mach-10 normal shock, both axial
flow directions, exact and two-cell checkerboard fronts, and 16, 32, and 64
radial cells. It checked positivity, exact planar preservation, directional
bypass, front position, and damping of imposed transverse errors.

- Result: `PASS`, 15/15 checks.
- Exact radial-uniform cases retained `u_r` and `omega_theta` at roundoff.
- The opposite-flow control and candidate plot trees were byte-identical.
- Candidate/control axis-vorticity ratios for the selected perturbed shock
  were 0.156, 0.214, and 0.683 at 16, 32, and 64 radial cells.

This proves that v2 can damp an imposed row-to-row mode in the ideal planar
problem. It is a necessary mechanism test, not evidence that the same face
treatment is consistent in the curved SRP Mach-disk field.

### Real SRP checkpoint

All branches restarted the same `chk40000`, advanced from step 40000 to 40250,
and ended at exactly `t=4.30332634858483 ms` on the same five-level AMR grid.
The executable uses characteristic LLF-WENO-Z5; it is not HLLC. The three
branches were:

| branch | R-Z Euler treatment |
|---|---|
| baseline | legacy path; annular average, point flux, gate, and v2 off |
| control | annular average + point flux + shock gate |
| candidate | control + directed transverse v2 first-order LLF face flux |

The baseline was run independently with `stop_time` pinned to the A/B endpoint.
Its Header contains the same time and level steps
`40250/80500/161000/322000/644000`.

## Bow-shock control

Relative to baseline, both modified branches have an L3 bow-position RMS of
0.000839 cells, a maximum shift of 0.00855 cells, median gradient ratio
0.999986, and fifth-percentile gradient ratio 0.997835. Candidate and control
are identical at the bow to roundoff because the directed flow-sign gate is
inactive there.

The bow control therefore passes. It does not rescue v2: the failed metrics
are specifically at the free, near-grid-aligned Mach disk.

## Interpretation

The data do not support a unique-root-cause claim yet. They do support these
narrower conclusions:

1. Exact planar R-Z preservation and checkerboard damping are not sufficient
   acceptance tests for the real Mach disk.
2. The annular/point-flux control already increases the first-ring axis
   artifact, so the next decomposition must isolate those mechanisms before
   modifying v2 further.
3. V2 replaces each selected transverse face with the complete first-order
   LLF/Rusanov flux. In a curved Mach disk with finite `u_r`, the abrupt
   high-order-to-first-order face switch can be inconsistent with the
   unchanged geometric pressure source and neighbouring reconstruction. The
   observed spike is consistent with that mechanism, but the current tests do
   not prove it is the only cause.
4. The candidate's flatter innermost front is not a valid repair because it is
   purchased by larger axis velocity and vorticity and does not remove the
   wider depression.

## Follow-up control decomposition

The frozen `chk40000` RHS was split into four branches with every unrelated
axis/shock correction disabled:

| branch | annular recovery | point flux | point gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| annular-only | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| annular + point | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| annular + point + gate | 1 | 1 | 1 |

The one-stage face audit contains 16,256 owned L4 radial faces. The directed
shock mask triggers on 510 faces and the point gate blocks 526; all 510
directed faces overlap. The 16 gate-only faces lie in a separate strong-jump
cluster near `z=-123 mm`, excluded by the incident-flow direction test. Both
masks span all 128 recorded radial columns. The first-eight-cell radial
momentum RHS increments are:

| isolated transition | RMS | absolute maximum |
|---|---:|---:|
| annular minus baseline | 1.20314e4 | 8.12154e4 |
| point minus annular | 4.50920e3 | 3.81736e4 |
| gate minus point | 1.01171e3 | 2.14518e4 |

Thus annular recovery is the largest immediate first-ring perturbation in this
snapshot. Point flux is second and the gate is smaller but nonzero. This is a
face-local causal decomposition, not by itself a claim that annular recovery
is the unique evolved root cause.

The four branches were then advanced 100 steps to exactly
`t=4.28608547574825 ms`. Equal step counts alone were rejected because the
first adaptive-CFL attempt ended at different physical times. At the pinned
endpoint:

| metric | baseline | annular | + point | + point + gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| center indent [mm] | 0.383705 | 0.383705 | 0.383380 | 0.383510 |
| first-8 front range [cells] | 0.05447 | 0.05483 | 0.05110 | 0.05166 |
| axis-window max `abs(u_r)` [m/s] | 1.72494 | 2.36618 | 2.35090 | 2.39642 |
| parity-aware axis max `abs(omega_theta)` [1/s] | 3750.62 | 4495.42 | 4581.47 | 4556.82 |
| first-8 max `abs(u_r)` [m/s] | 23.6422 | 23.7119 | 23.7299 | 23.7107 |

Annular recovery increases the short-window axis `u_r` metric by 37.2% and
the parity-aware axis vorticity by 19.9%. Adding point flux changes these by
-0.65% and +1.91%; adding the gate changes them by +1.94% and -0.54%.
None of the three mechanisms materially changes the existing broad
`0.384 mm` disk curvature in this short window.

Annular-only and gated point flux preserve the tracked bow front. In the
ungated point branch, 32 of 896 radial tracking rows switch between two
near-equal density-gradient peaks separated by exactly eight L3 cells; the
other rows have a 95th-percentile displacement of only `0.00588` cell. The raw
eight-cell maximum remains recorded, but is not interpreted as coherent
eight-cell bow motion. Enabling the gate removes every greater-than-one-cell
switch.

## Additive H-correction

The replacement-flux v2 path is not reused. On a directed, shock-triggered
radial face the new experiment retains the complete characteristic
LLF-WENO-Z5 flux and adds only

```text
delta_F_H = -0.5 * weight_H * alpha_r * (U_R - U_L)
```

where a smooth pressure-sensor ramp limits `weight_H` to the configured
strength. This is a shared Cartesian-face conservative increment. It is zero
for a radially uniform state, does not replace the pressure flux, and leaves
the axial shock-normal flux unchanged. It changes only Cartesian Euler face
fluxes and remains inside the pure shared-GP/full-Cartesian method contract.

The 18-case Mach-10 matrix tested exact and seeded shocks, both flow
directions, 16/32/64 radial cells, and strengths `0.125`, `0.25`, and `0.5`.
All structural checks passed. `0.125` is the only accepted strength; `0.25`
and `0.5` fail the all-resolution damping requirement. At `0.125`, the four
radial-velocity/vorticity damping ratios range from 0.450 to 0.930 across the
three grids. The opposite-flow candidate and baseline are byte-identical.

The real-SRP one-stage H audit also passes all 12 checks:

- the baseline and H branches have bitwise-identical base WENO fluxes;
- all 510 directed trigger faces receive the smooth additive correction,
  including 36 near-axis faces, while every non-triggered face receives zero;
- `F_selected=F_WENO+delta_F_H` has a maximum relative residual below
  `6e-17`;
- mean/max H weights are 0.08629/0.12449;
- the correction RMS is 6.8--11.1% of the corresponding complete
  LLF-minus-WENO flux difference, depending on component and radial window.

### Exact-time 250-step H gate

Both branches restarted `chk40000` and ended at exactly
`t=4.30332634858483 ms`. The H-off final plot tree is byte-identical to the
earlier independent baseline, confirming that the default-off code path is
unchanged.

| metric | baseline | H=0.125 | H/baseline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| center indent [mm] | 0.345104 | 0.343293 | 0.99475 |
| first-8 front range [cells] | 0.049288 | 0.044773 | 0.90840 |
| first-16 front range [cells] | 0.196474 | 0.191856 | 0.97650 |
| axis-window max `abs(u_r)` [m/s] | 1.41940 | 1.37500 | 0.96872 |
| first-8 max `abs(u_r)` [m/s] | 21.5492 | 21.4368 | 0.99478 |
| parity-aware axis max `abs(omega_theta)` [1/s] | 2536.172 | 2536.157 | 0.999994 |
| near-axis max `abs(omega_theta)` [1/s] | 9.89491e5 | 9.89345e5 | 0.999853 |

All nine frozen checks formally pass. However, the qualifying 9.16% reduction
acts on a baseline first-eight-cell range of only `0.04929` cell: the absolute
change is `0.00451` L4 cell, or approximately `0.705 micrometre`. Center
indentation changes by only `0.00181 mm`, axis radial velocity falls by 3.13%,
and parity-aware axis vorticity changes by only `0.00059%`. The ordinary
first-row derivative vorticity instead rises by 3.12%.

The result is not shock smearing: the Mach-disk axis gradient ratio is
0.999621, FWHM remains three cells, and the disk center moves only 0.00921 L4
cell. Bow position changes by at most `4.44e-13` L3 cell and its gradient
ratios are unity to roundoff. Density and pressure remain positive.

The full-field plots are visually indistinguishable, as these absolute changes
predict. The frozen gate was underpowered because a relative improvement of an
already negligible first-eight-cell range was allowed to qualify the
candidate. Its formal `PASS` is retained for provenance, but the engineering
decision is `NO-GO / no meaningful repair demonstrated`.

Do not spend a long AWS run on this exact `H=0.125` formulation. Do not select
a replacement axis treatment until the downstream recirculation is separated
from primary Mach-disk geometry. Annular recovery must remain disabled: the
decomposition identifies it as the largest independent near-axis perturbation.

## NPR15 shock-identity correction

The original NPR15 `chk05000` was advanced independently to the exact
historical `plt05098` time, `t=2.250413726331 ms`. The baseline reproduces the
historical density, pressure, radial velocity, and axial velocity fields with
relative L2 errors of `1.97e-13`, `2.63e-13`, `4.58e-13`, and `8.86e-14`.

The old detector selected the largest positive density gradient in each radial
row. At the axis it finds two compressions:

| feature | z/D | Mach before/after | density before/after [kg/m3] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| primary Mach disk | 2.39648 | 3.984 / 0.847 | 0.399 / 1.430 |
| downstream compression | 2.47557 | 0.448 / 0.244 | 1.348 / 2.505 |

The downstream peak is 1.111 times the primary peak in the first radial ring
and remains stronger through ring 2. At ring 3 the primary peak becomes
stronger. Connecting those row-wise maxima creates the artificial 20-cell
step. Comparing that line to `r/D=0.3--0.5` then crosses the barrel shock and
creates the reported 37-cell full range; neither value measures Mach-disk
flatness.

The corrected tracker selects the first compression with upstream Mach at
least 1.5 and downstream Mach at most 1.5:

| metric | baseline | H=0.125 | wb1 | wb2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| primary core range, `r/D<=0.08` [L4 cells] | 0.7676 | 0.7282 | 0.7618 | 0.7324 |
| primary first-8 range [L4 cells] | 0.2556 | 0.1999 | 0.2500 | 0.2226 |
| secondary/primary axis gradient | 1.1109 | 1.1274 | 1.1107 | 1.1158 |

Neither H nor the first-ring full-flux well-balanced probes materially alter
the already flat primary disk. H also slightly increases the relative
strength of the downstream peak.

Across the 30-frame statistics window, the physical disk's instantaneous core
range has median `1.45` L4 cells. The time average of instantaneous disk loci
ranges by `1.95` cells, while direct tracking in the online mean density field
ranges by `2.83` cells over `r/D<=0.08`. The original 32.8-cell mean-axis
claim is therefore withdrawn.

The downstream feature is nevertheless real in the computed field. At its
axis peak, axial velocity changes from approximately `+145 m/s` to `-89 m/s`;
it bounds a post-disk recirculation region. The current A/B evidence does not
tie it to the R-Z pressure source or transverse carbuncle mechanism. Its next
tests are radial grid refinement and a full-azimuth 3-D symmetry audit.
