# RZ-1 No-Solid Axisymmetric Euler Verification

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Status:** **PASS within the scope stated below**

## 1. Certified scope

This report verifies the no-solid axisymmetric Euler operator for:

- two-dimensional R-Z coordinates;
- a uniform, level-0 finite-volume mesh;
- single-species calorically perfect gas;
- LLF-WENO-Z5 spatial discretization;
- SSPRK(4,3) time integration;
- smooth solutions satisfying physical axis parity;
- cylindrical control-volume averages with the radial Jacobian `r`.

It does **not** certify GP-IBM, shocks, AMR, Navier-Stokes terms, an R-Z
positivity theorem, or an SRP configuration. The new R-Z path is opt-in,
default-off, and aborts in GPIBM or EB builds.

## 2. Discrete consistency defect and correction

The conserved state stored by an R-Z finite-volume cell is the annular average

\[
\bar{\mathbf U}_{ij}^{RZ}
=
\frac{\int_{C_{ij}}\mathbf U(z,r)\,r\,\mathrm dz\,\mathrm dr}
     {\int_{C_{ij}}r\,\mathrm dz\,\mathrm dr}.
\]

Directly applying `consToPrim` to this state treats an annular conservative
average as a point state. In general,

\[
p(\bar{\mathbf U}^{RZ})
\ne
p(\mathbf U(r_i,z_j)).
\]

For regular nonzero radial velocity, the unresolved radial kinetic-energy
variance produces an \(O(h^2)\) pressure defect. Multiplication by \(1/r\) in
the first annulus converts it into an \(O(h)\) radial-momentum residual.

The opt-in correction fits a quadratic polynomial to three conservative
annular averages using exact cylindrical moments of \(\{1,r,r^2\}\), evaluates
the polynomial at the radial cell centre, and only then converts the state to
primitive variables. The radial WENO flux and the geometric pressure source
use the same recovered centre state. A regular quadratic pressure field is
therefore balanced by the pressure-flux/source pair to roundoff.

Implementation locations:

- `src/rhs/RZFiniteVolume.h`: annular-average moment inversion;
- `src/rhs/Weno.h`: radial conservative-state deconvolution before WENO;
- `src/tim/compute_rhs.cpp`: matching centre pressure in the \(p/r\) source;
- `src/tim/advance.cpp`: independent audit of all SSPRK(4,3) FE brackets.

## 3. Verification problem

The time-dependent manufactured primitive fields are periodic in \(z\).
Density, pressure, and axial velocity are even in \(r\); radial velocity is odd
and satisfies \(u_r=0\) at the axis. Initial data, boundary data, source terms,
and the analysis oracle are fourth-order Gauss-Legendre cylindrical
control-volume averages.

The grid sequence is

\[
N_r=N_z=16,32,64,128,256.
\]

Three independent checks are used:

1. the complete semi-discrete Euler residual at \(t=0\);
2. a pressure-only metric/source balance oracle;
3. fixed-time SSPRK(4,3) evolution to \(t=0.02\) at CFL 0.1.

## 4. Semi-discrete results

The last two adjacent-grid orders of the volume-weighted \(L_1\) residual are:

| Conserved field | 64-128 | 128-256 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Density | 4.993 | 4.845 |
| Radial momentum | 1.997 | 1.999 |
| Axial momentum | 2.001 | 2.000 |
| Total energy | 1.995 | 2.000 |

The unusually high density order is specific to this manufactured field and
is not used to claim an order above two. The radial-momentum maximum error in
the axis cell converges with orders 2.404 and 2.258 on the last two grid pairs.
Over the first three annuli, the corresponding orders are 2.993 and 2.986.

For the regular pressure-only oracle, the largest radial-momentum \(L_\infty\)
error over all five meshes is

\[
4.0374\times10^{-13},
\]

which verifies the radial pressure-flux/geometric-source balance at roundoff.
The separate regular-axis regression also preserves `u_r` odd parity and the
even scalar fields; its linear radial-velocity case is roundoff limited, while
the cubic regular field converges at approximately second order.

## 7. Verdict and limitations

The no-solid, uniform-grid R-Z Euler operator passes RZ-1:

\[
\boxed{
\text{RZ-1 no-solid smooth Euler operator: PASS}
}
\]

Specifically, it demonstrates cylindrical cell-average consistency, axis
regularity, pressure-flux/source balance, approximately second-order complete
semi-discrete residuals, approximately second-order fixed-time SSPRK(4,3)
solutions, FE-bracket admissibility on the tested trajectory, and CPU/GPU/MPI
equivalence.

This result must not be transferred to the frozen pure GP-IBM. RZ-2 still
requires an independently verified `r`-weighted BI-CWLS target/support
functional, R-Z wall geometry and \(2\pi r\,\mathrm ds\) surface measures, and
full curved-wall convergence tests. Shock, AMR, and SRP qualification remain
separate gates.
